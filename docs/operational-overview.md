# Operational Overview

## Purpose

IaMob Helper was designed to solve a common problem in commercial operations: companies generate leads through paid media, landing pages, websites and WhatsApp, but the operational structure behind those leads is often fragmented.

The result is usually slow response time, manual qualification, inconsistent CRM updates, poor routing and loss of commercial opportunities.

The goal of IaMob Helper is to connect lead generation, AI qualification, WhatsApp automation, CRM workflows and operational tracking into a more structured and scalable commercial operation.

---

## The Operational Problem

High-volume lead operations usually face recurring bottlenecks:

- Leads arrive from multiple channels without a unified operational flow
- Sales teams depend on manual qualification before prioritizing opportunities
- CRM data is often incomplete, delayed or inconsistent
- Response time becomes slower as lead volume increases
- Marketing, sales and operations do not always work from the same data
- Commercial routing depends too much on manual decisions
- Managers lack real-time visibility into lead status, source and conversion quality

These problems reduce efficiency and make it harder to scale lead generation without increasing operational complexity.

IaMob Helper was created to reduce this gap between marketing acquisition and commercial execution.

---

## The Solution

IaMob Helper acts as an operational layer between acquisition channels, CRM systems, WhatsApp communication and sales teams.

The platform uses automation and conversational AI to:

- receive or process lead data
- start or support the first interaction
- qualify leads based on predefined business rules
- classify leads by intent and engagement
- update CRM records
- apply tags and operational status
- trigger routing rules
- support commercial follow-up
- create more visibility for managers

The system is not designed only as a chatbot.  
It is designed as a RevOps automation layer for commercial operations.
