# Lead Qualification Flow

## Objective

The qualification flow was designed to automate the first layer of commercial interaction while maintaining operational consistency and lead prioritization.

The system focuses on:
- reducing manual effort
- increasing response speed
- standardizing qualification
- improving routing accuracy

---

# Qualification Stages

## 1. Initial Contact

The lead enters through:
- Meta Ads
- Landing pages
- Website forms
- WhatsApp campaigns

The AI assistant starts the first interaction automatically.

---

## 2. Intent Identification

The conversational flow identifies:
- purchase intent
- product interest
- urgency level
- preferred contact channel
- engagement level

---

## 3. Qualification Logic

The lead is categorized into:
- Qualified
- Disengaged
- Finalized

Rules consider:
- engagement
- response quality
- buying intent
- scheduling interest
- specialist request

---

## 4. CRM Synchronization

The qualification result is synchronized with CRM operations through APIs and webhooks.

Actions may include:
- status updates
- lead routing
- tagging
- queue assignment
- operational triggers

---

## 5. Commercial Routing

Qualified leads are automatically routed to the appropriate commercial team or specialist.

Routing logic may consider:
- project interest
- urgency
- lead source
- operational queue
- commercial availability

---

# Operational Goals

- Faster response time
- Better lead prioritization
- Reduced manual workload
- Operational scalability
- CRM consistency
